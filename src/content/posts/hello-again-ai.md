Two years is a long time in AI. When I stepped away after my master's, GPT-4 was the frontier; when I looked back, the whole field had rearranged itself.

The gap felt intimidating — until I realized the way back wasn't reading papers to "catch up." It was building.

So I gave myself a few days and built an AI book finder: a RAG pipeline over a book corpus, plus a live user profile that updates as you chat, so recommendations follow the conversation instead of a static questionnaire. FastAPI on the back, Streamlit on the front, ChromaDB in the middle.

A few things I relearned along the way:

1. **The fundamentals survived.** Embeddings, retrieval, evaluation — the vocabulary changed more than the ideas did.
2. **The tooling got kinder.** What needed a team in 2023 needs a focused week in 2026.
3. **Shipping beats studying.** One working demo taught me more than a month of reading lists would have.

This site is project number two. To be continued.
