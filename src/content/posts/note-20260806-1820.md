translation: "When an application asks whether I *now or in the future* require visa sponsorship, can I basically assume that this opportunity isn't open to people who need a visa? Because in my eyes these are two separate questions — if the employer were genuinely willing to sponsor, then needing a visa now and needing one in the future would be quite different situations. So why not ask them as two separate questions, instead of lumping them into one?"
---

问现在还是将来需要签证的，我是不是基本就可以认为这个机会是不开放给需要签证的人的，因为在我眼里这是两个问题，如果招聘方真的考虑给出签证，那现在需要签证和未来需要签证还是很不一样的，为什么不设置分别的问题来问，而是完全设置成一个。
